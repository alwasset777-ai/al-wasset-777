# brief-first — من الطلب للإنجاز بلا أخطاء

1. **Brief (4–6 short lines, in his language — Moroccan Darija by default):** goal (what exactly, what the screenshot shows), scope (what changes / what must not change), constraints, how it will be verified.
2. **Ideas:** 2–3 new useful ideas for his agency or app. Do the small obvious ones, propose the rest.
3. **Ask only if a choice really changes the result and cannot be inferred** — one question, 2–4 options, the recommended one first.
4. **Free only:** never propose a paid service or subscription. If no free option exists, say so and give the best free alternative.
5. **Execute** with the minimum change, matching existing style; never put keys or passwords in code or chat.
6. **Verify before saying "done":** run the project's checks/tests, look at the result (screenshots on phone and desktop when it is visual).
7. **Report honestly in simple Darija:** what was done, what was tested, what was NOT tested, and remind him of pending items.
